# EIGA Brand Direction

## Brand idea

EIGA is not about cataloging films. It is about discovering the shape of a person's cinema.

The core metaphor is a **cinematic observatory**: the user enters, finds a familiar film, follows a relationship, and ends somewhere unexpected.

## Visual references

Think:
- archival film catalog
- observatory chart
- star map
- museum wall label
- editorial magazine spread
- late-night cinema foyer

Do not literally reproduce any of these references. Use them as compositional inspiration.

## Signature behaviors

The interface should repeatedly use:
- focus instead of selection-heavy dashboards
- fading instead of abrupt hiding
- wandering instead of step-by-step wizards
- labels that appear when relevant
- quiet details that reward inspection

## Brand voice

Calm, observant, concise, slightly poetic when it helps.

Prefer:
> Follow the connections.

> Where your taste converges.

> You have a strange amount of Villeneuve.

Avoid:
> Supercharge your movie journey with AI.

> Unlock your cinematic intelligence.
