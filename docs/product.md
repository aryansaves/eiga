# EIGA Product Brief

## One sentence

Turn your Letterboxd history into an interactive map of your movie taste.

## Core emotional response

> I want to see mine.

## Audience

Primary:
- Letterboxd users
- film nerds
- people with enough viewing history to have patterns
- visualization / data-art enthusiasts
- people who enjoy film rabbit holes

Secondary:
- people who want beautiful yearly movie summaries
- film clubs / friend groups
- people who enjoy sharing personal visualizations

## Product distinction

Letterboxd answers:
> What have I watched?

EIGA answers:
> How is what I've watched connected?

## Primary journey

Landing page → demo graph → import Letterboxd export → local parse → personal universe → focus a node → explore outward → share/export.

## V1

- polished interactive demo
- Letterboxd CSV import
- browser-local processing
- movie nodes
- director relationships
- search
- zoom/pan
- hover/selection/focus
- movie detail surface
- basic derived statistics

## Later candidates

- actors
- genres
- decades
- timeline
- rabbit-hole mode
- graph paths
- cinematic hubs
- taste clusters
- yearly maps
- PNG/SVG export
- optional metadata enrichment
- optional saved public universes

## Explicitly not the product

- Letterboxd replacement
- review platform
- generic social network
- infinite recommendation feed
- AI movie critic
- watchlist manager
- content subscription service

## Prototype success criteria

A stranger can:
1. understand EIGA in under ten seconds,
2. use the demo without signing up,
3. import their data without a tutorial,
4. discover something surprising,
5. want to show another person their map.
